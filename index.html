OpenRight

Send links to the right app. For Mac.

Click a Spotify, Zoom, Apple Music, Apple Podcasts or Apple TV link anywhere on your Mac (Mail, Messages, Slack, a document) and it opens straight in that app, with no browser detour and no download prompt. Every other link goes to your usual browser, unchanged.

You can also send any website to an app of your choice, and have songs open in your preferred music service.
